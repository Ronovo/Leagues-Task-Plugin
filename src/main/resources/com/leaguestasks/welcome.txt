Welcome to Leagues Tasks.

This plugin brings the Old School RuneScape Leagues task format to the main game.

A Leagues event gives you a large curated checklist of goals across skilling, bosses, quests, and regions -- this plugin lets you work through that same kind of checklist on any account, at any time.

Its task list is a curated set drawn from past Leagues events, gathered into one list you can filter, roll from at random or pick from yourself, and check off as you complete tasks.

See "About Plugin" for how the plugin works, and "Tracker App" for the optional companion website.
