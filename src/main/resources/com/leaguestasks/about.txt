About Plugin

Leagues Tasks is a standalone RuneLite plugin. The task list ships as a
bundled resource and all of your progress is saved locally through
RuneLite's own config, so the plugin needs no server of its own.

Works on any account type:
- Existing main accounts
- Ironmen of all flavors
- Task-locked fresh accounts

The flow:
- Browse the task list and filter by region, difficulty, and activity type.
- Add a task to your list two ways: assign a specific task yourself from its detail page, or use Pick Random Task to roll one from the filtered pool. Your list holds one task per difficulty tier, beginner through master.
- Mark tasks complete by hand, or use Autocomplete Tasks to mark off what the client can verify for itself, on confirmation. Nothing is marked without you asking.
- Completions are tracked per account, so an alt never overwrites a main's progress.

Optional sync: if you run the Personal RuneScape Tracker web app, the
Sync Progress screen can push your progress to it. Sync is opt-in, needs
a server URL and token in the plugin settings, and only sends data
outward -- nothing from the tracker changes your local progress. See the
"Tracker App" page for details.

A sync sends your character name and account hash, your quest completion
states, your achievement diary and combat achievement progress, your
completed-task list, and -- only when you capture it from the in-game
Collection Log -- your collection log. Nothing is sent until you enter a
server URL and token and press a Sync button.
